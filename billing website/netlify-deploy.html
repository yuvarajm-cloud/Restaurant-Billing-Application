✨ Recommended: Netlify is the easiest way to deploy your website. Just drag and drop!

Step 1: Go to Netlify

Visit https://app.netlify.com/drop

Step 2: Sign Up (Free)

Create a free account - it takes 30 seconds

Step 3: Drag & Drop

Drag your entire billing website folder onto the Netlify page

Step 4: Get Your Link!

Netlify will give you a URL like: your-restaurant.netlify.app

Share this link with anyone - they can access your website!
